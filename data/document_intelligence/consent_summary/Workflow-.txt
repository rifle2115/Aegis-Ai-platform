Workflow-
User Upload (PDF/Text)
          │
          ▼
  PDF/Text Extraction
  - Convert PDF to plain text
  - Remove headers/footers/page numbers
          │
          ▼
     Preprocessing
  - Split text into sentences/paragraphs
  - Clean punctuation, line breaks
          │
          ▼
Sentence Classification / Extraction
  - Keyword Matching:
      e.g., risk, injury, liability, mandatory, duration
  - Sentence Embeddings (BERT, RoBERTa):
      Find sentences semantically related to core categories
  - Optional Classifier:
      Label sentences as Risk / Obligation / Duration / Liability / Signature
          │
          ▼
   Summarization / Structuring
  - Extractive Summary:
      Select top 1–2 sentences per category
  - Optional Abstractive Summary:
      Use BART/T5 to rewrite into concise plain-language summary
          │
          ▼
Output: Structured Summary
  Example:
  --------------------------------
  Adventure Consent Form Summary
  - Risks: Participant may experience injuries from falls or equipment misuse.
  - Obligations: Helmets must be worn; follow instructor rules.
  - Duration: Activity lasts approximately 3 hours.
  - Liability: Organization not responsible for accidents from rule violations.
  - Consent: Participant signature required.
  --------------------------------
